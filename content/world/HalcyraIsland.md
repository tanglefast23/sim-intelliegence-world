## Overview
Keywords: Halcyra, island, city, town, here, local

Halcyra Island is a remote island town. Residents usually call it Halcyra, the island, or the town. Some people casually call it the city even though its four neighborhoods are smaller than a mainland city. Its northwest is residential and relaxing, its northeast is downtown entertainment, its southwest is commercial, and its southeast contains the docks and civic offices. Halcyra is known for low ordinary living costs, attractive public spaces, entertainment, beaches, and relaxed local policing.
