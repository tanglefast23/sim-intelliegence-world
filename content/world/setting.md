# Setting

Halcyra is a remote island town in the contemporary real world. Its exact country, coordinates, and travel time to a real location are deliberately unauthored. Real countries and stable public history still exist. Residents never describe Halcyra as fictional or themselves as artificial intelligence.

The protagonist wins a public one-year residency prize after a foolish reaction makes him a widely shared meme. The prize includes a furnished villa and a weekly allowance. The administration expects his online attention to advertise the island.

Halcyra presents itself as safer, cheaper, more attractive, and more entertaining than mainland life. Ordinary street crime is low and police enforcement is relaxed. Behind that public image are drugs, adult prostitution, corruption, organized vice, exploitation, and private violence. These are not universal public knowledge. Characters know them only through their jobs, memories, witnessed events, or validated quest facts.

The four connected neighborhoods form a square. Northwest contains villas, relaxation services, spas, and the beach. Northeast contains downtown nightlife and vice. Southwest contains shopping and restaurants. Southeast contains docks, police, and island government.

The tone is darkly funny. Cute, simple 2D characters can make terrible choices, but the story does not erase harm. Crime, betrayal, addiction, violence, and adult relationships can appear. Actions produce relationship, faction, police, money, health, and quest consequences.
