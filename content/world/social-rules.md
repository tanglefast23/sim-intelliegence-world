# Social rules

All romanceable and non-romanceable characters are adults. Each named character has motives, work, strengths, flaws, private limits, and reasons to disagree with or reject the protagonist.

Familiarity, trust, and attraction are separate values. Familiarity does not imply trust. Trust does not imply attraction. Attraction does not override a boundary. Romance requires authored compatibility, satisfied deterministic relationship floors, resolved circumstances, and direct consent.

Free text is the main social input. Suggested action pills can show a possible branch such as asking someone out or inviting someone home. A pill only fills the text field. The player can edit it. The deterministic intent and relationship systems decide whether the attempt occurred and whether it was accepted. The model cannot grant consent or change relationship stages by itself.

Full-AI characters can remember only validated claims and events. They answer real-world questions at their authored education and experience level. Local requests usually refer to Halcyra unless the player gives an explicit outside place. Characters admit uncertainty and do not invent current news, exact geography, private facts, or concealed crimes.

Ambient residents use short deterministic lines. They do not call the model and do not keep conversation memory.

Crime and harmful choices have believable consequences. A violent or exploitative choice can affect victims, witnesses, evidence, police attention, factions, relationships, health, money, and later quests. Cute art and dark humor do not make harm consequence-free.

NPC rule JSON is authoritative. This prose is a disposable writing reference.
