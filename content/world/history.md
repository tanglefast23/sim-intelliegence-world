# History

Halcyra grew around a sheltered harbor, a civic dock, and private villa development. Tourism and service businesses later expanded into four compact neighborhoods. The island administration sells a clean public story: low costs, beaches, attractive public spaces, entertainment, and light policing.

The current administration created a one-year promotional residency prize. Officials selected the protagonist after a careless public reaction made him a widely followed meme. They judged him easy to advertise and unlikely to inspect their claims carefully. The selection is publicly described as luck, not as a calculated publicity decision.

The Velvet Tide developed as a concealed network around nightlife, favors, intimidation, and illegal trade. Its name and detailed operations are not common public facts. A character must have an authored connection, direct evidence, or a validated report before treating those details as true.

The prototype begins on the first morning of the promised year and does not need to reach the one-year ending. If a later version reaches that date, the residency outcome must be authored as a major branch, not inferred by the model.

This prose does not change deterministic state.
